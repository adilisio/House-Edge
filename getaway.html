House Edge

Heist night. The getaway.

Dee drives. You hold the map and call each turn before she reaches the junction. Get her to the garage in the top corner.

  • Left, Straight, Right are from Dee's seat, not the map. Heading down the map, her left is your right.
  • Call early. A last-second call makes her brake hard, and that's noise.
  • Patrol cars see straight along their street. Roadblocks go up ahead of you, with a moment's warning.

Being seen raises the heat. If it maxes out, you ditch the car and run.
